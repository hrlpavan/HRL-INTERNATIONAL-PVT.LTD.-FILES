### 1. Why Pre-Incorporation is the Smartest Engineering Strategy

Startups that incorporate before acquiring clients face a well-known financial trap:
- **Statutory Audit Burden**: Even with zero revenue, a registered Private Limited company must appoint an independent Chartered Accountant (CA) and file audited financial statements (Form AOC-4 and MGT-7) with the Ministry of Corporate Affairs, costing ₹25,000 to ₹50,000+ each year.
- **GST Administrative Drain**: Once registered for GST, an entity must file 12 monthly GSTR-1 returns, 12 monthly GSTR-3B returns, and 1 annual GSTR-9 return—regardless of whether any invoice was raised. Missing a single deadline incurs daily compounding financial penalties.
- **Advance Tax & Corporate Overhead**: Minimum alternate tax (MAT) calculations and formal corporate filings burn valuable time and runway that should be spent serving clients.

By operating lean, **HRL International operates with near-zero unnecessary administrative burn**, passing cost savings directly to our clients and dedicating 100% of our focus to engineering excellence.

### 2. The Statutory Exemption Thresholds (CGST Act, 2017)

Section 22 of the Central Goods and Services Tax (CGST) Act, 2017 explicitly states:
> *"Every supplier shall be liable to be registered under this Act in the State or Union territory... from where he makes a taxable supply of goods or services or both, if his aggregate turnover in a financial year exceeds twenty lakh rupees."*

Therefore:
- Service providers with an annual turnover under **₹20,000,000 (₹20 Lakhs)** are legally exempted from GST registration.
- Invoicing clients prior to this threshold without GST is **completely compliant, legal, and recognized by the Government of India**.
- **Bonus for Clients**: Clients save the 18% GST surcharge on software development services during this initial phase!
