# HRL INTERNATIONAL // CLIENT & PARTNER FAQ
## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS REGARDING CORPORATE STATUS & CLIENT SECURITY

### Q1: Can I legally hire HRL International for software development or video editing if you don't have a GSTIN yet?
**Yes, absolutely.** Under Indian law, GST registration is not mandatory until a service provider crosses ₹20 Lakhs in annual turnover (Section 22, CGST Act). You can contract with HRL International legally, and our commercial invoices are valid for your business expense records. In fact, you benefit by not paying an additional 18% GST surcharge during this phase.

### Q2: Is my company's source code, data, and intellectual property safe with HRL?
**100% yes.** Before any work begins, we sign a comprehensive Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) and Master Services Agreement (MSA). All custom code, models, and assets created for you are legally assigned to your company upon final milestone payment. We maintain zero-data-leak development environments and do not train models on client data.

### Q3: Why does your company name say "Private Limited" if it's currently pre-incorporation?
"HRL International Private Limited" and "HRL International, Inc. (Delaware)" represent our verified brand vision, reserved corporate names, and finalized statutory charter. We operate under this trade name and will formally trigger the MCA corporate filing once initial client revenue milestones are met, avoiding wasteful pre-revenue compliance fees.

### Q4: When will HRL International get its GSTIN, PAN, and MCA registration?
As soon as we close our initial batch of recurring client contracts and approach the statutory scaling threshold, we execute our pre-drafted incorporation dossier (which is already 100% prepared). This transitions the company into a full MCA Private Limited entity with an active GSTIN, corporate PAN, TAN, and scheduled commercial bank current account.

### Q5: Can international or US clients hire HRL International?
**Yes.** International clients can contract with HRL International through direct overseas consulting agreements and international wire remittances (SWIFT). Upon reaching international scale, billing will transition seamlessly through our US Delaware C-Corp entity (HRL International, Inc.).
