# HRL INTERNATIONAL // CLIENT SECURITY & IP CHARTER
## CONFIDENTIALITY, CODEBASE INTEGRITY & INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY ASSIGNMENT

**EFFECTIVE DATE**: 2026  
**APPLICABLE TO**: All Clients, Enterprise Partners, Sponsors & Commercial Collaborators  
**JURISDICTION**: Republic of India & International Commercial Law  

---

### 1. The Core Guarantee
HRL International guarantees that any code, data, business plan, technical specification, or media asset shared with us is treated with **absolute confidentiality and institutional-grade security**.

We do not train public models on client data. We do not recycle proprietary client architectures. We do not disclose client identities or deliverables without explicit prior written authorization.

---

### 2. IP Assignment Policy (100% Client Ownership)
1. **Custom Work Product**: Any code, algorithm, shader, web application, script, or documentation created specifically for a client under a Statement of Work (SOW) is the **exclusive intellectual property of the client upon final settlement of the agreed invoice**.
2. **Pre-Existing Frameworks**: If HRL utilizes its pre-existing open-source or proprietary runtimes (e.g. RTX-LocalAI modules or base OFX utilities), the client is granted a perpetual, royalty-free, non-exclusive commercial license to utilize, modify, and distribute the software.
3. **No Lock-In**: Deliverables are handed over with complete source code, compilation instructions, and zero vendor lock-in.

---

### 3. Technical Security Safeguards
- **Private Repositories**: Client work is conducted inside encrypted, access-restricted private repositories (e.g., GitHub private organization repos).
- **Clean Room Development**: Core algorithms and client implementations are segregated across sandboxed development workstations with strict zero-leak policies.
- **Hardware Isolation**: GPU workloads and video rendering pipelines run locally on dedicated Apple Silicon and NVIDIA RTX hardware, ensuring zero third-party cloud scraping.

---

### 4. Non-Disclosure & Breach Remedies
Any breach of confidentiality is actionable under:
- **Indian Contract Act, 1872** (Breach of trust & contractual liability)
- **Information Technology Act, 2000** (Cyber security, data protection & computer tampering)
- **Commercial courts and arbitration tribunals** with full liquidated damages.
