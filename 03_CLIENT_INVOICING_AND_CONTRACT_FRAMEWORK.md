# HRL INTERNATIONAL // CLIENT INVOICING & ENGAGEMENT FRAMEWORK
## CONTRACTUAL AGREEMENTS, MILESTONE BILLING & LEGAL RECOGNITION

---

### 1. Engagement Workflow
Onboarding a project with HRL International follows a streamlined, institutional 4-step process:

1. **Discovery & Scope Definition**: Technical requirement gathering, architecture drafting, and deliverables timeline.
2. **Mutual NDA & Master Services Agreement (MSA)**: Both parties execute a legally binding confidentiality and development agreement.
3. **Statement of Work (SOW)**: Detailed milestone breakdown, acceptance criteria, and fee schedule.
4. **Milestone Execution & Delivery**: Phased code releases with staging demonstrations, culminating in final handover and IP assignment.

---

### 2. Invoicing Standard (Phase 1 Pre-GSTIN)
During Phase 1, invoices issued by HRL International contain:
- Invoice Reference Number (e.g., `HRL-INV-2026-001`).
- Date of Issuance and Milestone Reference.
- Detailed Description of Services / Deliverables.
- Total Amount Payable in INR (for Indian clients) or USD (for international clients).
- **Statutory Notice**: *"Services provided under statutory exemption threshold of Section 22 of the Central Goods and Services Tax Act, 2017 (Aggregate turnover under ₹20,00,000)."*
- Direct Bank Account Details for NEFT / RTGS / IMPS / Wire Remittance.

---

### 3. Payment Terms & Security
- **Milestone Distribution**: Typical projects are structured into 30% upfront initiation, 40% mid-project verification, and 30% final deployment and IP transfer.
- **Client Peace of Mind**: Code is demonstrated live on staging environments or video walk-throughs before final payment releases.
