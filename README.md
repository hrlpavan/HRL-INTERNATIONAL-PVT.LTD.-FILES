## 1. Executive Summary & Operating Stance

This public repository serves as the transparent, binding corporate policy of **HRL International** regarding our operational model, client data security, and statutory compliance roadmap.

### The Lean Startup Doctrine: Why We Operate Pre-Incorporation
Many early-stage startups rush into incorporating a Private Limited company, acquiring a GSTIN, and taking on complex corporate tax registrations before establishing predictable, recurring client revenue. In practice, this creates **premature capital drain**:
- Mandatory annual statutory audit fees (₹25,000 to ₹50,000+ annually, even with zero revenue).
- Mandatory monthly/quarterly GST returns (GSTR-1, GSTR-3B) with recurring accountant fees and late-penalty risks.
- Advance corporate tax overhead and corporate compliance fees with zero business benefit.

**Paying taxes, government compliance fees, and legal retainer costs for zero revenue is a direct financial loss.** HRL International refuses to waste capital on unnecessary bureaucratic overhead during early development. Instead, 100% of our resources and energy are invested where it matters most: **engineering world-class software, high-throughput GPU runtimes, and delivering exceptional value to our clients.**

## 2. Legal Legality & Statutory Exemption Under Indian Law

Operating as a pre-incorporation enterprise / proprietary technology firm is **100% legal, recognized, and protected under Indian and international commercial law**:

1. **GST Exemption (Section 22 of CGST Act, 2017)**:  
   Under Indian tax law, any business entity or professional service provider with an aggregate annual turnover **under ₹20 Lakhs** is legally **EXEMPT** from GST registration. Operating without a GSTIN at this stage is fully compliant with the Ministry of Finance and Central Board of Indirect Taxes and Customs (CBIC).
2. **Indian Contract Act, 1872**:  
   All commercial agreements, Statements of Work (SOW), and Master Services Agreements (MSA) executed by the Founder & MD on behalf of HRL International are legally binding, enforceable contracts under Indian courts.
3. **Information Technology Act, 2000**:  
   Protects all digital transactions, electronic records, source code confidentiality, and cyber security assets.
